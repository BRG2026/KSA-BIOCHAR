About Us

Our Mission

Saudi Biochar is at the forefront of transforming waste management in Saudi Arabia. We convert solid waste, agricultural residues, and livestock waste into high-quality biochar through advanced pyrolysis technology.

Our biochar enhances soil health, captures carbon permanently, and supports Saudi Vision 2030's sustainability goals. We're building a circular economy where waste becomes a valuable resource for environmental protection and agricultural growth.
